Major Accomplishments

CR26I rear axle oscillation diagnosis

CR26I's rear axle was oscillating at the car's natural frequency. I retuned the damper setup to settle it.

ResultLateral-g oscillation amplitude cut by 56%, helping the team place 6th (2025) and 8th (2026) overall at FSAE Michigan.

Redesigned the bumpstops

I redesigned the suspension bumpstops from SBR rubber to a 3D-printed TPU part and tested 54 samples.

ResultManufacturing deviation cut by 83%, from 192.7 to 32.5 lbf/in.

Designed and verified the geometry package

I designed the suspension geometry package and set performance targets for camber, toe, and caster across the full travel range, then measured the built car with a FARO arm.

ResultMotion ratios within 1.3% of design.

Standardized driver feedback

I designed and deployed setup sheets capturing the Cooper-Harper handling qualities scale alongside tire pressures, camber, and toe, so setups could be compared apples-to-apples across test days instead of relying on driver memory.

ResultDriver feedback and tire data used together to validate geometry choices against how the car actually drove.
