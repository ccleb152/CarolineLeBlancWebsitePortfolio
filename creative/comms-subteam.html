Building the Comms Subteam

Crimson Racing's communications work used to be one person doing everything. I built it into a subteam with its own structure.

When I took over, communications was a single role with no real handoff process, whoever held it just carried everything in their head. I grew it into a subteam of four and built out separate systems so the work doesn't disappear when someone graduates.

  • Lead a subteam of 4, mentoring members directly in Adobe software so design knowledge transfers between graduating classes instead of resetting every year.
  • Built out separate systems and workflows for the areas that used to live in one person's head: content, outreach, and print production.
  • Boosted engagement through outreach events, recruitment initiatives, and social media campaigns.
  • Designed and managed all digital and print communications: social content, website updates, and university outreach materials including flyers and promotional graphics.

The same translation skill shows up off the team too: as EDAG Student Team Lead on our capstone, I presented our manufacturing facilities guideline directly to German clients, turning engineering work into something a non-technical stakeholder could act on.
